Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you choose to give us, including:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage information, such as login activity, needed to keep your account secure.

We do not collect sensitive financial details such as bank account numbers or card numbers, since Spendly is a manual expense log and does not connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve the Service. This includes:

  • Displaying your expenses, categories, and spending summaries back to you.
  • Maintaining your account and authenticating your sign-ins.
  • Diagnosing bugs and improving the reliability of the Service.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. While we take reasonable steps to protect stored data, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on limited third-party services for core functionality, such as hosting infrastructure or font delivery. These providers only process the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided within the application.
